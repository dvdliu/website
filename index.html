My Story

I'm a recent University of Michigan graduate with a B.S.E. in Computer Science (2025). I came into college with a general passion for engineering and problem solving, and eventually found myself drawn to computer science through building small apps and games based on fun ideas I had.

I really fell in love with programming after taking the operating systems and distributed systems classes at UMich, where I got hooked on how existing systems work under the hood — and how much of systems design is about intentional trade-offs and careful engineering decisions. One of my biggest motivations is that I really enjoy learning, and continuing to build on that is something I want to carry forward in my career.

What I'm Doing Now

At Whova, I work on the registration system, focusing on integrations between Whova and external platforms to build features and services that help customers connect their event workflows across different apps. I also work on payment auditing and monitoring to improve system reliability and observability.
