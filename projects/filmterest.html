Overview

Filmterest is a social movie discovery platform that matches users based on their film and TV preferences, using the Letterboxd API and OAuth 2.0 for authentication. It started out as a learning exercise to pick up React, Node, and JavaScript from scratch, and grew into a full app for sharing movie reviews and discovering films with friends.

Users connect their Letterboxd account, and the app pulls their watch history and ratings to surface people with overlapping taste — think of it as a matchmaking layer built on top of your film diary. Under the hood it's a full-stack app: a React/Redux frontend, an Express API, and MongoDB for storing user profiles, match state, and review data.

Tech Stack
React Redux Express MongoDB OAuth 2.0 Letterboxd API
